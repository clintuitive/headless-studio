**The Headless Studio** is about making records with code. Real VST3 amp sims,
hardware drum machine samples, sample instruments, convolution rooms — all of
it driven from Python scripts, with the scores, source assets and render
settings kept alongside so the work can be revised a year later instead of
admired from a distance.

None of it is theoretical. Everything published here was pulled out of a
working pipeline that has produced [two complete albums](/music.html) —
Sign-Off and The Quiet Hours, both playable in full on this site.

## The book

The articles are the short version: one idea each, the load-bearing tricks,
written for people who already write code for a living.

**The Headless Studio** — the book — is the long version, and it takes the
gentler road. It's written for an intermediate programmer with no audio
background and only surface-level Python, and it explains every idiom and every
audio concept the first time it turns up. If the articles move too fast, that's
not you; that's the format.

It's **complete and free** — all 15 chapters and 5 appendices,
[readable in full here](/book.html): hosting plugins headlessly, rescuing the
incompatible ones, building and mapping your own sample instruments, synthesis
from a handful of sine waves, arranging as data, performance timing, mixing,
and processed float stems verified against the working mix. The entire pipeline
is [free and open on GitHub](https://github.com/clintuitive/headless-studio)
under the MIT license.

No paywall, no catch — take it, use it, make something. If it saves you an
afternoon and you feel like it, you can
[buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/clintjohnson). Always optional.
